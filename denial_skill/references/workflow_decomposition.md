# ClaimGuard Hierarchical Workflow Decomposition

This file is the required phase and atomic-step map. Each atomic step includes the fields specified in `claimguard_skill_decomp_prompt.md`.

## Executive Summary

The workflow converts a denial letter, EOB, ERA, or payer notice plus available records into a source-grounded provider-staff work product: case checklist, routing decision, deadline controls, evidence packet, draft appeal or corrected-claim packet, submission record, follow-up plan, escalation path, outcome posting, and prevention insight. The model assists with extraction, classification, routing, gap detection, citation-grounded drafting, and tracking. Humans retain authority over deadlines, clinical judgment, payer submissions, legal escalation, and PHI handling.

## Phase Map

| phase_id | phase_name | purpose |
|---|---|---|
| P01 | Intake | Create case, preserve source documents, and capture basic claim context. |
| P02 | Extraction | Extract and tag denial facts with provenance and confidence. |
| P03 | Classification | Determine payer/plan type, denial type, and operational category. |
| P04 | Authority and Privacy | Confirm provider authority, representative status, consent, AOB, and HIPAA controls. |
| P05 | Deadlines | Calculate appeal, response, follow-up, and escalation deadlines. |
| P06 | Records and Policy Gathering | Request missing payer materials, plan terms, policies, claim file, and clinical records. |
| P07 | Evidence | Build clinical, administrative, coding, authorization, and network evidence packet. |
| P08 | Strategy | Select corrected claim, reopening, reconsideration, peer-to-peer, appeal, expedited appeal, external review, complaint, fair hearing, or legal escalation. |
| P09 | Drafting | Draft appeal, correction, reopening, or escalation packet. |
| P10 | Quality Control | Verify facts, citations, deadlines, PHI, signatures, and packet completeness. |
| P11 | Submission | Submit through payer-required channel and preserve proof. |
| P12 | Follow-Up | Track payer response deadlines, call logs, and next actions. |
| P13 | Escalation | Analyze upheld denials and route to next level. |
| P14 | Outcome Posting | Verify reprocessing, payment, balance correction, and case closure. |
| P15 | Prevention Feedback | Convert root cause into policy library, template, training, and analytics updates. |

| P01-S03 | Preserve source provenance | Intake specialist | Case created | Original files, OCR output, metadata | Store immutable originals, OCR text, checksum or version ID, page numbers | Every extracted fact must map to a source document and page/segment | Provenance index | `source_indexed_at` | OCR drift, overwritten originals, no page references | Generate provenance table and confidence flags | Staff verifies originals are preserved |

| P03-S01 | Determine payer and plan type | RCM analyst | Payer-plan facts extracted | Plan card, EOB, eligibility, SPD/EOC, payer portal, contract | Classify as commercial fully insured, self-funded ERISA, Marketplace/ACA, Medicare FFS, Medicare Advantage, Medicaid managed care, other government, workers' compensation, or unknown | Use explicit source evidence; if unknown, create verification task | Plan type classification | `plan_type_verified_at` | Misrouting ERISA, MA vs FFS confusion, Medicaid fee-for-service vs MCO confusion | Infer likely type and list evidence gaps | Human confirms plan type before deadline calculation |
| P03-S02 | Determine denial type | RCM analyst | Denial reasons extracted | Denial reason, codes, policy text, claim data | Classify denial type: medical necessity, experimental/investigational, prior authorization, level of care, setting, OON/network adequacy, coding/bundling/modifier, missing documentation, timely filing, eligibility/COB, benefit exclusion, duplicate, contractual adjustment, patient responsibility, or administrative defect | Primary type drives first action; secondary types must still be answered | Denial type classification | `denial_type_classified_at` | Over-classifying as appeal when corrected claim is needed, missing secondary rationale | Map CARC/RARC and narrative to categories | Staff validates category before strategy |
| P03-S03 | Detect urgency and ongoing-care risk | Clinical liaison | Pre-service, concurrent, termination, reduction, or urgent clinical facts appear | Denial letter, orders, chart notes, clinician statement, service schedule | Determine whether standard timing may seriously jeopardize life, health, maximum function, or severe-pain avoidance; detect Medicaid continuation-of-benefits or concurrent-care issue | Urgent if clinician support and facts meet controlling standard; Medicaid continuation if prior authorized service is reduced/terminated and timing conditions may apply | Urgency and continuation flag | `urgency_detected_at`; `service_effective_date` | Missing urgent route, unsupported urgency claim, missing continuation request deadline | Draft clinician question set and urgency summary | Clinician/provider confirms urgency and continuation request |
| P04-S01 | Validate provider appeal authority | RCM analyst | Plan and denial type classified | Provider contract, payer manual, EOB/denial instructions, AOB | Determine whether provider can appeal directly, needs assignment, or needs patient/member authorization | If authority is missing or uncertain, gather required authorization before submission | Authority checklist | `authority_verified_at` | Provider files without authorization, wrong representative form | Identify authority gaps from documents | Staff/legal/compliance verifies authority rules |
| P04-S02 | Validate patient consent and representative status | Intake specialist | Appeal may require patient authorization | AOB, authorized representative form, HIPAA authorization, payer form, CMS-1696 if Medicare | Confirm forms are present, current, signed, and match payer requirements | Missing form blocks filing unless provider has independent appeal right | Representative/consent artifact list | `authorization_due_at`; `authorization_verified_at` | Expired or unsigned form, wrong patient, wrong appeal level | Generate missing-form request checklist | Staff verifies signatures and payer acceptance |
| P04-S03 | Apply HIPAA minimum-necessary controls | Compliance reviewer | Any packet, draft, training example, or external sharing | PHI inventory, purpose, recipient, attachments | Limit PHI to appeal purpose; redact unrelated records; avoid PHI in model training examples unless de-identified or approved | Include only appeal-relevant identifiers and records | PHI handling note | `phi_review_completed_at` | Sending full chart unnecessarily, copying unrelated diagnoses, PHI in logs | Identify unnecessary PHI and suggest redactions | Compliance or trained staff verifies PHI handling |
| P05-S01 | Calculate appeal filing deadlines | RCM analyst | Plan type and denial date available | Denial date, received date, plan type, payer stated deadline, rule table, state/payer rules | Calculate source-stated and rule-derived filing deadlines; apply earliest plausible deadline when conflicts exist | Every deadline needs source, calculation, confidence, and verification flag | Deadline table | `appeal_deadline`; `deadline_confidence` | Using denial date instead of receipt date, ignoring weekends/state rules, missing earliest deadline | Calculate multiple deadline scenarios with assumptions | Human verifies final deadline before submission |
| P05-S02 | Calculate payer response due dates | RCM analyst | Appeal/reconsideration/external review route selected | Filing date, route, plan type, rule table, payer acknowledgement | Calculate decision due date and follow-up cadence | Response due date depends on route, urgent status, plan type, extensions, and tolling | Response due-date table | `payer_response_due_at`; `follow_up_due_at` | Wrong clock start, missing extension, not tracking IRE transfer | Generate response timeline | Staff verifies with payer acknowledgement |
| P05-S03 | Create deadline safeguards | Case owner | Any deadline exists | Deadline table, staff calendar, task system | Add reminders before and after filing and response deadlines; escalate imminent deadlines | High-risk if deadline within 10 days, unknown receipt date, urgent care, Medicaid continuation, or high dollar value | Calendar/tasks and escalation flag | `reminder_at`; `escalation_at` | No reminder, stale queue, no backup owner | Prioritize cases by deadline risk | Supervisor validates high-risk deadline controls |
| P06-S01 | Request missing payer materials | RCM analyst | Denial reason or policy basis incomplete | Denial letter, plan contact, authorization status | Request EOB/denial, plan document/SPD/EOC, payer medical policy, claim file, clinical criteria, internal guideline/protocol, coding rationale, PA records, call logs, and all records reviewed | Required when denial rationale is incomplete, medical judgment involved, or ERISA/ACA appeal needs full review | Payer-materials request | `payer_materials_requested_at`; `payer_materials_due_at` | Failing to request criteria, late request, no proof | Draft request letter and call script | Staff confirms request channel and logs proof |
| P06-S02 | Request missing provider records | Clinical liaison | Evidence gaps detected | EHR, orders, notes, tests, imaging/labs, operative/procedure notes, therapy history, auth proofs | Request and collect records tied to each denied service-line criterion | Evidence must map patient facts to denial rationale and policy criteria | Provider-records request list | `records_requested_at`; `records_received_at` | Missing key DOS note, wrong patient, unindexed records | Generate record gap list by denial type | Clinical staff verifies record relevance |
| P06-S03 | Collect plan and policy sources | Policy analyst | Appeal strategy requires policy mapping | Payer policy, plan terms, LCD/NCD, Medicaid handbook, coding manuals, specialty guidelines, literature | Store current policy version, effective dates, URL/source, and criteria text | Must be current for DOS and denial/adjudication context; stale policies require warning | Policy source library entry | `policy_effective_date`; `policy_verified_at` | Citing stale policy, using wrong plan product, inventing criteria | Compare policy criteria to facts | Human verifies policy currency and applicability |
| P07-S01 | Build clinical evidence packet | Clinical liaison | Medical necessity, level of care, experimental, setting, urgent, or OON denial | Physician LMN, chart notes, orders, tests, imaging/labs, procedures, conservative therapy, FDA indication, specialty guidelines, peer-reviewed literature | Assemble evidence and map each item to a policy criterion or denial rationale | Include only supported, relevant, verified clinical facts | Clinical evidence matrix | `clinical_packet_ready_at` | Unsupported clinical claims, missing clinician sign-off, excess PHI | Draft criterion-to-fact matrix | Clinician verifies clinical facts and medical necessity |
| P07-S02 | Build administrative evidence packet | Billing analyst | Auth, timely filing, eligibility, COB, OON, network, or missing-doc denial | Authorization proof, eligibility verification, call logs, fax receipts, portal screenshots, timely filing proof, network search evidence | Assemble administrative proof and timeline | Evidence must show what happened, when, who confirmed it, and reference numbers | Administrative evidence matrix | `admin_packet_ready_at` | No proof of submission, vague call notes, missing reference numbers | Build timeline and gap list | Staff verifies source documents and call logs |
| P07-S03 | Build coding and corrected-claim evidence | Coding specialist | Coding, bundling, modifier, revenue code, diagnosis, duplicate, or clerical denial | Claim lines, coding rationale, payer edits, NCCI or payer rules, corrected claim data | Determine whether coding support, corrected claim, reopening, or appeal is appropriate; prepare corrected data if needed | If payer did not adjudicate due to fixable defect, prefer correction/reopening while preserving appeal rights | Coding support or correction packet | `coding_review_completed_at` | Appealing fixable coding defect, omitting modifier support, duplicate resubmission | Suggest likely correction vs appeal path | Certified coder or billing lead verifies |
| P08-S01 | Select first action | Case owner | Classification, authority, deadline, and evidence status available | Denial type, plan type, deadlines, payer instructions, evidence gaps | Route to corrected claim, reopening, reconsideration, peer-to-peer, formal appeal, expedited appeal, external review, complaint, fair hearing, or legal escalation | Route must preserve rights and answer controlling denial reason | Routing decision memo | `route_selected_at`; `route_deadline` | Peer-to-peer used after deadline, appeal skipped, external review premature | Apply routing decision tree | Manager verifies route for high-risk cases |

| P08-S03 | Define requested remedy | Case owner | Appeal theory selected | Claim data, patient balance, requested services, payment objective | Specify requested action: authorize service, overturn denial, reprocess claim, pay service line, correct patient balance, continue benefits, expedite decision, provide peer reviewer | Remedy must match payer authority and denial stage | Requested remedy statement | `remedy_defined_at` | Vague remedy, wrong payer stage, asking for unavailable relief | Draft concise remedy options | Staff verifies remedy with operational objective |
| P09-S01 | Draft appeal or request packet | RCM analyst | Strategy and evidence available | Template, evidence matrices, source facts, rule citations, attachments | Draft letter and packet with identifiers, denial summary, requested action, facts, criteria mapping, rebuttal, medical necessity/admin/coding argument, attachment index, review requests | Draft must distinguish known, inferred, and missing facts | Draft packet `draft_for_human_review` | `draft_created_at` | Hallucinated facts, copied irrelevant PHI, missing denial reason | Draft structured appeal letter | Human must review before use |

| P09-S03 | Draft external review or fair-hearing packet | RCM analyst | External review, IRO, Medicaid hearing, or next-level appeal selected | Final adverse determination, prior appeal packet, deadline table, authorized representative forms | Draft request with final denial, grounds for eligibility, requested review, evidence index, and representative forms | Eligibility and timing must match state/federal/Medicare/Medicaid rules | External/next-level packet | `next_level_packet_created_at` | Premature external review, wrong state process, missing final determination | Generate next-level checklist | Compliance/legal/staff verifies route and forms |
| P10-S01 | Fact and source QA | Quality reviewer | Draft packet complete | Draft packet, source documents, extracted fact table | Compare all dates, codes, names, claim numbers, amounts, policy quotes, and attachments to source | No unsupported facts may remain unflagged | QA discrepancy log | `fact_qa_completed_at` | Wrong service line, wrong DOS, unsupported statement | Produce source-to-draft trace | Reviewer signs off or returns corrections |
| P10-S02 | Denial-rationale coverage QA | Quality reviewer | Fact QA underway | Denial reason table, draft packet | Verify every denial rationale and code is answered directly | Any unanswered rationale blocks submission | Rationale coverage checklist | `rationale_qa_completed_at` | Appeal answers only medical necessity but misses auth/coding/timeliness | Build coverage matrix | Reviewer confirms all rationales answered |
| P10-S03 | Deadline, authority, and PHI QA | Quality reviewer/compliance | Packet near final | Deadline table, forms, PHI handling note, payer instructions | Verify filing deadline, response deadline, representative authority, consent/AOB, payer channel, signatures, and PHI minimization | Missing authority, missed deadline, or excess PHI blocks submission | Final QA sign-off | `final_qa_completed_at`; `submission_due_at` | Wrong fax, missing signature, full chart included, deadline unverified | Generate blocker list | Human sign-off required |
| P11-S01 | Submit by required channel | Case owner | Final QA approved | Final packet, payer instructions, portal/fax/mail info, forms | Submit using required payer channel; follow payer naming, form, and attachment requirements | Channel must match denial letter, plan, payer portal, or contract | Submission record | `submitted_at` | Wrong channel, portal timeout, missing attachment | Generate submission checklist | Staff performs and confirms submission |

| P13-S03 | Request missing rationale or claim file after uphold | RCM analyst | Unfavorable response lacks rationale or reviewed materials | Final denial, prior requests, plan rules | Request missing rationale, claim file, reviewer criteria, policy, and records reviewed | Required when final adverse determination is incomplete or next-level argument needs record | Claim-file/rationale request | `post_uphold_request_at` | Escalating without record, missing clinical criteria | Draft request and evidence gap note | Staff verifies request and response |
| P14-S01 | Verify overturn and reprocessing | Payment poster/AR specialist | Favorable or partial favorable response | Appeal response, remittance, payment posting, patient ledger | Confirm claim reprocessed, payment issued, contractual adjustment correct, denial removed, and patient balance corrected | Case cannot close until payment/ledger effect is verified or non-payment follow-up is open | Payment verification record | `payment_verified_at` | Closing on approval without payment, wrong patient balance | Compare response to remittance/ledger | Payment staff verifies posting |

## Minimum Output Contract

Every generated case response must include:

- facts grouped as `known_from_documents`, `inferred`, and `missing_needs_human_verification`;
- plan type, denial type, and first-action route with confidence and evidence;
- deadline table with source-stated, calculated, and verification status;
- evidence gap checklist;
- draft appeal or alternative packet outline;
- follow-up plan;
- human review gates.
