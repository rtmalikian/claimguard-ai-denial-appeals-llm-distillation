# ClaimGuard Appeal Letter Template

Mark every generated letter as `DRAFT - FOR CLINICIAN/PROVIDER AND DEADLINE REVIEW BEFORE SUBMISSION`.

## Front Matter

```text
[Date]

[Payer/Plan Appeal Department]
[Appeal address, fax, portal, or other required channel]
[If portal submission: include portal name and case/reference field]

Re: Appeal of Denial / Request for Reconsideration / Redetermination / External Review
Patient/Member: [Name] | [Member ID] | [DOB only if necessary]
Subscriber/Group: [Subscriber name if needed] | [Group/Plan ID]
Provider/Facility: [Name] | NPI [NPI] | TIN [TIN if required]
Claim Number / ICN / DCN: [Identifier]
Dates of Service: [Dates]
Denied Service(s): [CPT/HCPCS/revenue code/modifier/units]
Diagnosis Code(s): [ICD-10 codes if relevant]
Authorization Number: [Auth number or "not available"]
Denial Date / Notice Date: [Date]
Appeal Level: [Internal appeal, expedited appeal, Medicare redetermination, etc.]
Requested Action: [Authorize service / overturn denial / reprocess and pay claim / correct patient balance / continue benefits]
```

## Opening Request

```text
This is a draft appeal submitted for human review. On behalf of [provider/patient/member, authority source], we request review and reversal of the denial for [service/procedure] for [patient/member] on [date(s) of service]. We request [specific remedy] and written confirmation of the decision.
```

If expedited handling is requested:

```text
We request expedited handling because, based on the treating clinician's attached statement, applying the standard timeframe could [seriously jeopardize the patient's life/health/ability to regain maximum function or expose the patient to severe pain]. The clinical basis for urgency is summarized below and documented in Attachment [X].
```

## Short Denial Summary

```text
The denial notice dated [date] states that [payer denied/not authorized/refused payment] because [payer-stated rationale]. The denial cites [policy/criteria/code] and identifies appeal instructions requiring submission by [deadline/channel], according to [source].
```

Separate payer-stated reasons:

| denial reason | source | response section |
|---|---|---|
| [Reason 1] | [Denial letter page/paragraph or CARC/RARC] | [Section] |
| [Reason 2] | [Source] | [Section] |

## Concise Clinical Facts

Only include facts supported by records and verified by clinician/provider.

```text
[Patient/member] has [diagnosis/condition] documented in [record and date]. Relevant clinical facts include:

- [Fact 1] ([source record/date]).
- [Fact 2] ([source record/date]).
- [Prior conservative therapy/history/test result, if relevant] ([source]).
- [Functional impairment, risk, or treatment need, if relevant] ([source]).
```

Unsupported or inferred clinical points must remain in a reviewer note, not the final letter.

## Medical Necessity Argument

Use only when supported by clinician review.

```text
The treating clinician has reviewed this appeal and states that [service] is medically necessary for [patient/member] because [clinician-verified reason]. The evidence supporting medical necessity includes [records, test results, guideline/policy mapping]. The requested service is [consistent with plan criteria / consistent with cited policy / supported by attached guideline or literature] for this patient's documented condition.
```

## Administrative, Coding, Authorization, or Timeliness Argument

Use the relevant subsection only.

### Coding or Modifier

```text
The billed code/modifier combination is supported by [operative note/procedure note/coding reference/payer policy]. The denial appears to rely on [coding rationale], but [record-based response]. If the payer requires correction rather than appeal, please process the attached corrected claim data or advise in writing without waiving appeal rights.
```

### Prior Authorization

```text
Authorization was requested/obtained on [date] under [authorization number/reference]. The attached records show [approval scope, service, provider, dates, call reference, portal screenshot]. If the payer contends authorization was invalid, please identify the exact missing or nonmatching element and all records reviewed.
```

### Timely Filing

```text
The claim/appeal was timely because [date submitted] was within [verified rule/contract deadline], supported by [clearinghouse report/fax receipt/portal confirmation/certified mail receipt]. If the payer calculates a different deadline, please provide the contractual or plan provision relied upon and the calculation.
```

### Experimental or Investigational

```text
The denial characterizes the service as experimental/investigational. The attached evidence addresses that conclusion: [FDA indication if applicable], [specialty guideline], [peer-reviewed literature], and [patient-specific rationale]. Please have the appeal reviewed by a specialist with appropriate expertise in [specialty].
```

### Out-of-Network or Network Adequacy

```text
The denial states that the provider/service was out of network. The attached evidence shows [emergency status / no available in-network provider / network search results / continuity-of-care issue / plan exception]. Please apply the applicable network exception or identify in writing the in-network providers available to furnish the service within the required timeframe and clinical capability.
```

### Eligibility or Coordination of Benefits

```text
The attached eligibility/COB evidence shows [coverage active / primary payer / COB update]. Please update the eligibility/COB record and reprocess the claim. If the denial is maintained, please identify the eligibility data source, effective dates, and records reviewed.
```

## Requested Remedy

```text
For the reasons above and based on the attached records, we request that [payer/plan] reverse the denial, [authorize/pay/reprocess] [service/claim/service line], correct any associated patient responsibility, and issue a written decision explaining the basis for the determination.
```

Optional requests:

```text
We also request:

- a reviewer with appropriate specialty expertise;
- a peer-to-peer discussion with [clinician] if allowed;
- the complete claim file, medical policy, clinical criteria, internal guideline/protocol, coding rationale, and records reviewed if the denial is upheld;
- preservation of all external review and next-level appeal rights.
```

## Evidence Packet Checklist

- Denial letter, EOB, ERA, or RA with all pages.
- Plan document, SPD, EOC, member handbook, provider manual, payer medical policy, LCD/NCD, or Medicaid/Medicare rule when applicable.
- Claim form and service-line detail.
- Authorization request, approval/denial, portal screenshots, call logs, and reference numbers.
- Eligibility and COB verification.
- Physician letter of medical necessity.
- Chart notes for relevant dates of service.
- Orders, test results, imaging, labs, operative/procedure notes.
- Conservative therapy history and failed alternatives when relevant.
- FDA indication, specialty guideline, and peer-reviewed literature when relevant.
- Network search/access evidence for out-of-network or network adequacy issues.
- Coding support, modifier rationale, corrected claim data, and payer coding guidance when relevant.
- Timely filing proof: clearinghouse reports, payer acknowledgement, fax receipts, portal confirmations, certified mail receipts.
- Authorized representative form, AOB, patient consent, or CMS appointment form as required.
- Final QA checklist and proof-of-submission plan.
